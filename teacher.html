Classroom Controls

Sign in

Sign in with your district Google account to control the headsets in your classroom.

Headsets never sign in and never send anything back. They only receive the settings you choose here.

Headset link

Put this link in ArborXR (WebXR Link). Every headset opened with it follows this page. Use a different room code for each class or space.

Freeze screens

In passthrough, the stone disappears and students see the room plus your message. Freezes end on their own after 2 hours.

Send everyone to a dot

Opens that dot's panel on every headset (works in Detective mode too).

Settings

“Link” means each headset keeps whatever its ArborXR link says. Students can change unlocked settings in Options until you change that setting again.

Safety check

Tests the database rules live: that strangers can’t send commands, that nothing but this room can be read, and that only the allowed values get through.
