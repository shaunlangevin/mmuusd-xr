Safety check

Tests the live database rules: that strangers can't send commands or pause headsets, that nothing but this room can be read, and that only the allowed values get through. Run it after you publish new rules.

    Everyone

    Freezes every headset with a calm message. In passthrough the stone disappears so students see the room. Press Resume to continue. Freezes end on their own after 2 hours.

    Guided tour

    You lead everyone through the dots. Students can't open other dots or turn the stone, so everyone's stone stays lined up; they can still Listen, Hide and move panels (if allowed). With Notice first on, each step opens with a look-closely prompt: let them talk, then Reveal reading (playing the narration reveals it too). Walking students get an arrow to the dot; seated or table-size stones turn to face them. Headsets that join late jump to the current step.

    The tour is off, so students explore on their own.

    Pause one headset

    The number is the sticker on each headset. Tap to pause just that headset ("Your teacher paused this headset"); tap again to resume. Pauses end on their own after 2 hours.
