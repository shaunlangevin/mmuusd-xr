Classroom Controls · Cannon Recoil Lab

Sign in

Sign in with your district Google account to control the lab on the headsets in your classroom.

Headsets never sign in and never send anything back. They only receive the settings you choose here.

The teacher console isn't connected to a Firebase project yet, so the headsets run the lab on their own (that works fine).

To turn it on, follow firebase/SETUP.md in the repository, then fill in js/firebase-config.js.

Everyone

A message fills every headset in this group and the lab waits underneath, so nothing is lost. Press Resume to go back.

Start over clears every headset's lab for the next pair of students. Nothing was saved, so nothing else is affected.

The lab

Guided (the default, about 8 minutes): one step at a time. Students try the cannon, answer a Newton's 3rd law question, then compare a light and heavy setting of one variable and explain the difference. Built so pairs can work without help.

Full investigation: students choose a variable, test every level three times, fill a data table and take on the target challenge. Start at Plan skips the opening for a second day.

Variable: which variable the guided comparison uses (Default is cannon weight). In the full investigation it fixes the variable for everyone (Default lets students choose).

Changing any of these starts every headset over.

Supports for every headset

Students can also turn these on themselves in Options. For one student, use Supports for one headset below.

Fire button replaces pulling the cord with a button on the panel (for students who find the grab hard).

Pause one headset

Numbers are the stickers on the headsets, never students. A paused headset shows "Your teacher paused this headset." Tap again to resume it.

Supports for one headset

For a student with an accommodation: give that headset's number bigger text (A+), calm mode, numbers on the ruler (123), the Fire button (Btn) or Spanish (ES). They add to the supports for everyone.

Room and link

Every headset uses the same ArborXR link. A headset's number (picked once on the lab's start page, and shared with the exhibits) decides which group it follows.

For a one-off group, type a room code, and give those headsets a link ending in ?room= plus that code.
